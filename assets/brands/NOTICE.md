# Brand artwork sources

- LinkedIn, GitHub, and Chrome paths: [Font Awesome Free](https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome), icons licensed under CC BY 4.0. Copyright Fonticons, Inc. Original license is retained in FONT-AWESOME-LICENSE.txt. Paths are embedded, scaled, and recoloured in generated cards.
- Gmail and technology logos: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0. Original license is retained in SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt. Paths are embedded, scaled, and recoloured.
- Maskr: Vaibhav's published extension logo from its [official Chrome Web Store listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/maskr-private-ai-leak-gua/adncldmdaamhlpcccigfhdipjhpicebe). Embedded without changing the image.

Exact download URLs are recorded in sources.json. The surrounding header, cards, charts, and layouts are original SVG artwork.
